# Aegis Lens Community Code of Conduct

*Version 1.0 — 2026-05-30*

## Our Pledge

The Aegis Lens community — on Discord, Telegram, GitHub, and community forums — is a space for conflict intelligence research, open-source OSINT collaboration, and knowledge sharing. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender, nationality, political opinion, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Standards

**We encourage:**
- Fact-based, source-cited discussion of conflict events
- Constructive feedback on data quality, methodology, and tool limitations
- Supporting newcomers learning OSINT and conflict analysis
- Reporting potential misuse or data integrity concerns privately to safety@aegislens.com

**We do not tolerate:**
- Doxxing, or sharing personal information about private individuals
- Requests for tactical targeting data or real-time positions of military units
- Coordinated information operations or deliberate disinformation
- Harassment, threats, or abusive language toward any person or group
- Content that could provide direct tactical uplift in active conflict
- Sharing CSAM or graphic imagery without appropriate warnings and justification
- Commercial solicitation or spam

## Platform-Specific Rules

### Discord
- Keep discussion in the correct channel
- No cross-posting identical messages to multiple channels
- Use `/report` for urgent safety issues

### Telegram
- Bot commands only in designated channels
- No forwarding of unverified rumours without the "UNVERIFIED" label

### GitHub
- Issues and PRs must be in English
- No spam issues or duplicate bug reports
- Sensitive security bugs: email security@aegislens.com, do not open a public issue

## Enforcement

### Strike System

| Action | First offence | Second offence | Third offence |
|--------|--------------|----------------|---------------|
| Minor violation (off-topic, mild language) | Warning | 7-day mute | Permanent ban |
| Doxxing attempt | Immediate ban | N/A | N/A |
| Tactical uplift request | Immediate ban | N/A | N/A |
| Spam | 24-hour mute | Permanent ban | N/A |
| Harassment | 30-day ban | Permanent ban | N/A |

### Appeals

Appeal a moderation decision within 14 days by emailing community@aegislens.com with:
- Your username and platform
- The action taken and date
- Why you believe it was incorrect

Appeals are reviewed by a moderator who was not involved in the original decision.

### Reporting

Report violations to:
- Discord: DM a moderator or use `/report`
- Telegram: @aegis_mod_bot
- Email: community@aegislens.com (for sensitive reports)

All reports are confidential. We do not share reporter identities.

## Crisis Moderation

During major events (large-scale attacks, sudden escalations):
- Additional moderators join all channels
- Unverified information must be labelled `[UNVERIFIED]`
- Sharing real-time positions or targeting data is grounds for immediate permanent ban
- Crisis protocol activated by any senior moderator

## Transparency

We publish a quarterly moderation summary with:
- Number of warnings, mutes, and bans (aggregated)
- Number of reports received and acted upon
- Any policy updates

---

*This Code of Conduct applies to all Aegis Lens community spaces. For questions: community@aegislens.com*
