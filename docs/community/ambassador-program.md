# Ambassador Program

> **Status:** v1.0 — operational policy. Review semi-annually.

Aegis Lens ambassadors are regional power users who evangelize the platform in their local community, language, and professional network. A single great ambassador in Kyiv, Warsaw, or Berlin delivers more qualified reach than any paid ad campaign in the same city.

---

## 1. What Is an Ambassador?

An **Aegis Lens Ambassador** is a trusted community member who:

- Actively uses the platform and can speak to its value from firsthand experience.
- Is willing to represent Aegis Lens at local events, online, and to their professional network.
- Takes on a regional focus (country or city cluster) and operates primarily in the local language.
- Commits to a **minimum 6-month tenure** with quarterly reviews.

Ambassadors are **not** paid employees. They are recognized power users with elevated access and a meaningful voice in product direction.

---

## 2. Ambassador Application

### Who can apply

- Level 2+ Contributor (see `docs/community/contributor-program.md`) **or** an established professional in journalism, OSINT, NGO, or defense research with a public track record.
- Active in the platform for at least 60 days.
- Located in or deeply familiar with the target region.

### Application process

1. Fill out the Ambassador Application at `/community/ambassadors/apply`.
   - Fields: name/alias, region, professional background, links (LinkedIn / Twitter / published work), motivation statement, proposed activities (events, articles, community building).
2. **Automated check:** account standing, no active bans, contributor tier.
3. **Review** (ops + community team, 14 business days): async interview (30-min video call or written Q&A).
4. **Decision:** Approved → onboarding package sent; Declined → email with specific feedback + 6-month reapplication window.
5. **Max ambassadors per region:** 3 for large countries (UA, PL, DE, US), 2 for medium (UK, RO, Baltics), 1 for smaller regions.

## 4. Monthly Office Hours with the Team

- **Format:** Group video call (Zoom / Google Meet), 60 minutes.
- **Frequency:** Monthly, same weekday each month (announced in `#ambassadors` 10 days prior).
- **Agenda (rotating):**
  - 15 min: Product roadmap update + preview
  - 20 min: Ambassador spotlight (one ambassador shares a campaign or insight)
  - 15 min: Open Q&A + issue escalation
  - 10 min: Upcoming events / coordination
- **Recording:** Shared in `#ambassadors` within 24h for those in conflicting time zones.
- **Minimum attendance:** 6 of 12 office hours per year to remain active.

---

## 5. Quarterly Review of Ambassador Impact

At the end of each quarter, each ambassador submits a **Quarterly Impact Report** (template in `#ambassadors` channel):

| Metric | Target (illustrative) |
|---|---|
| Events organized or spoken at | ≥ 1 |
| New community members referred | ≥ 10 |
| Content published (articles, threads, talks) | ≥ 2 |
| Enterprise / institutional leads generated | tracked (no minimum) |
| Platform feedback items submitted | ≥ 3 |

**Review outcomes:**
- **Active:** Impact targets met → perks renewed for next quarter.
- **At risk:** 1 quarter below targets → coaching conversation, support plan.
- **Inactive / exit:** 2 consecutive quarters below targets, or voluntary exit → see §8.

## 6. Per-Region Playbook for Outreach

Each ambassador receives a **Region Playbook** tailored to their geography. Core sections:

### 6.1 Target audiences by region

| Region | Priority audiences |
|---|---|
| 🇺🇦 Ukraine | Journalists, civic-tech orgs, NGOs, military analysts (within ethical bounds) |
| 🇵🇱 Poland | Diaspora journalists, think tanks, border NGOs, university security studies programs |
| 🇩🇪 Germany | Policy researchers, investigative media, security & intelligence academics |
| 🇺🇸 United States | OSINT communities, newsrooms (Bellingcat ecosystem), defense policy orgs |
| 🇬🇧 United Kingdom | Think tanks (RUSI, Chatham House networks), BBC / Channel 4 data journalists |
| 🌍 Baltics / Romania | Government & NATO-adjacent researchers, cyber units, regional media |

### 6.2 Approved outreach activities

- Speaking at OSINT / investigative journalism conferences.
- Hosting local meetups (virtual or in-person) — budget covered up to $500/quarter.
- Publishing tutorials, case studies, or platform walkthroughs on personal channels.
- Academic collaborations (workshops, student challenges).

### 6.3 What ambassadors must not do

- Claim to speak on behalf of Aegis Lens in sensitive editorial or policy matters without prior approval.
- Share internal roadmap details not yet publicly announced.
- Use the platform to assist in operations that could endanger sources or individuals.
- Offer discounts or special pricing not authorized by the sales team.

## 7. Ambassador-Led Local Meetups Budget

- **Request process:** Submit a meetup brief (date, format, venue, expected attendance, proposed agenda) to ops@aegislens.io at least 21 days in advance.
- **Budget cap:** $500/quarter per ambassador. Unused budget does not roll over.
- **Eligible expenses:** venue hire, catering, printed materials (Aegis Lens–branded), A/V equipment.
- **Reimbursement:** receipts submitted within 30 days post-event via Expensify / bank transfer.
- **Co-promotion:** Aegis Lens promotes approved meetups on official channels (blog, social, email digest).

---

## 8. Exit & Handover Protocol

### Voluntary exit

- Ambassador notifies ops@aegislens.io with ≥ 30 days notice.
- Final quarterly report submitted.
- Handover document created: active contacts, open leads, upcoming events, community context.
- Ambassador badge retained as honorary status; Enterprise access expires after 30 days.
- Exit interview offered (optional, 30 min) — feedback used to improve the program.

### Involuntary exit (policy violation or sustained inactivity)

- Two written warnings minimum before exit, except for Code of Conduct violations (immediate exit).
- Enterprise access revoked immediately upon exit decision.
- Public communications about the exit are neutral: "X has stepped down as Ambassador."
- Handover document still required (7 days notice minimum).

### Overlap period

During a handover, the outgoing and incoming ambassador (if any) have a 2-week overlap facilitated by the ops team to transfer contacts and context.
