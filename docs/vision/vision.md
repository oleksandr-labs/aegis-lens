# Aegis Lens Vision

## Executive Summary

**EN:** Aegis Lens is building the world's most trusted open-source intelligence platform for conflict monitoring and geopolitical analysis — combining real-time multi-source data ingestion, AI-powered verification, and a collaborative analyst community to transform raw information chaos into actionable, citable intelligence. Our north star: become the Bloomberg Terminal of geopolitics, a neutral standard used by journalists, researchers, humanitarian organisations, and policymakers worldwide.

**UK:** Aegis Lens створює найнадійнішу у світі платформу відкритої розвідки для моніторингу конфліктів та геополітичного аналізу — поєднуючи збір даних з множини джерел у реальному часі, ШІ-верифікацію та спільноту аналітиків для перетворення хаосу необробленої інформації на дієві, цитовані дані. Наша полярна зірка: стати Bloomberg Terminal геополітики — нейтральним стандартом, що використовується журналістами, дослідниками, гуманітарними організаціями та урядовцями по всьому світу.

## What We Won't Do

Aegis Lens is built on trust. The following are hard editorial and ethical boundaries:

1. **No misinformation amplification.** We will never publish unverified claims as fact. All events carry a confidence score. Low-confidence events are clearly labelled.
2. **No targeting of individuals.** We do not track, profile, or expose private individuals. Public figures and state actors operating in their official capacity are in scope; civilians are not.
3. **No weapons facilitation.** Data will not be packaged or positioned to assist in weapons targeting, even under commercial pressure.
4. **No propaganda.** Editorial decisions are made by human editors using documented methodology. AI assists; humans decide.
5. **No paywall on humanitarian data.** Core conflict data used for civilian protection and humanitarian response will remain accessible to verified NGOs and journalists at no cost.
6. **No black-box AI.** Our AI models are described in public methodology notes. We disclose what we can verify and what we cannot.
7. **No data brokering.** User behaviour data is not sold or shared with third parties outside of GDPR-compliant service providers.
