# Aegis Lens — Public Vision

*This is the public-facing version of our vision document, suitable for the /about/mission page and external communications. It reflects our mission, user impact goals, and technical ambitions.*

---

## Our Mission

Aegis Lens exists to make open-source intelligence accessible, verifiable, and trustworthy — so that journalists, researchers, humanitarian workers, and informed citizens can understand the world's conflicts with clarity and confidence.

**UA:** Aegis Lens існує для того, щоб зробити розвідку з відкритих джерел доступною, верифікованою та надійною — щоб журналісти, дослідники, гуманітарні працівники та небайдужі громадяни могли розуміти конфлікти у світі з ясністю та впевненістю.

---

## What We're Building

A real-time intelligence platform that aggregates, verifies, and contextualises information from hundreds of open sources — satellite imagery, social media, wire services, flight tracking, maritime AIS, and more — into a single, searchable, citable map.

We combine:
- **Real-time data ingestion** from dozens of source types
- **AI-powered verification** to score source credibility and flag inconsistencies
- **Human expert review** so every published event carries a documented confidence level
- **Open methodology** so our process can be critiqued, improved, and trusted

**UA:** Ми будуємо платформу розвідки в реальному часі, яка агрегує, верифікує та контекстуалізує інформацію з сотень відкритих джерел — супутникові знімки, соцмережі, новинні агенції, трекінг польотів, морський AIS тощо — в єдину карту з можливістю пошуку та цитування.

## Who We Serve

- **Journalists and editors** — verifying breaking news, tracking frontline movements, understanding context
- **OSINT researchers and analysts** — professional-grade tools for conflict investigation
- **Humanitarian organisations** — understanding where people are in danger and where aid is needed
- **Policy and academic researchers** — structured, citable data for papers and reports
- **Informed citizens** — understanding what is happening and what has been verified

## Our Technical Ambitions

### Near-term
- Comprehensive monitoring of Ukraine and adjacent regions with sub-one-hour event latency
- AI Copilot that lets analysts query the entire event graph in plain language
- Public live map widely embeddable by any newsroom or researcher

### Medium-term
- Coverage of every active conflict worldwide
- Predictive crisis index: a composite, model-driven signal that flags escalation risk before it becomes headlines
- Eight languages, serving analysts across Europe and beyond

### Long-term
- The global standard for open-source conflict intelligence — cited in academic papers, referenced by international bodies, embedded in newsrooms on every continent
- A public API ecosystem allowing thousands of researchers, developers, and civil-society organisations to build on our data

---

## Our Principles

1. **Verification first.** We do not publish what we cannot substantiate. Confidence levels are always disclosed.
2. **Open methodology.** Our processes are documented, critiqueable, and continuously improved.
3. **Neutral by design.** We report events; we do not advocate for outcomes.
4. **Privacy by default.** We track conflicts, not people. Civilians are not data points.
5. **Accessible to those who need it most.** Verified journalists and humanitarian organisations access core data free of charge.

---

## What We Won't Do

- Amplify unverified claims or speculation as fact
- Target, profile, or expose private individuals
- Package data in ways that could facilitate weapons targeting
- Operate as a propaganda tool for any state or non-state actor
- Sell user data to third parties
