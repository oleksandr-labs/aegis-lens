## Quarterly Review Schedule

### Q1 Review Checklist (end of Month 3)
- [ ] Phase 1 MVP features shipped and publicly accessible
- [ ] First 50 region pages indexed by Google
- [ ] AI Copilot activation rate measured (target: >40% of new users try it)
- [ ] At least 1 data journalism outreach sent to top-10 press outlets
- [ ] Source count: Telegram (target: 200+), RSS (target: 100+)
- [ ] CWV scores: all green in Search Console
- [ ] Vision document reviewed by founder; metrics updated

### Q2 Review Checklist (end of Month 6)
- [ ] 500+ paying analysts reached or on track
- [ ] Enterprise pilot #1 signed or in final negotiation
- [ ] NGO partnership term sheet signed
- [ ] ADS-B + AIS data flowing
- [ ] UK locale shipped
- [ ] First press citation confirmed (any tier-1 outlet)
- [ ] Backlink profile: 50+ referring domains
- [ ] Vision document reviewed; 1-Year targets updated with actuals

### Q3 Review Checklist (end of Month 9)
- [ ] 3 enterprise pilots confirmed
- [ ] 1 NGO partnership publicly announced
- [ ] 10k MAU (on track to 50k)
- [ ] First public dataset + DOI published
- [ ] 3rd locale in progress (PL or DE)
- [ ] SOC 2 readiness assessment completed
- [ ] Vision 3-Year targets stress-tested against current trajectory

### Q4 Review Checklist (end of Month 12)
- [ ] 50k MAU target assessment (hit / miss / revised)
- [ ] Annual progress report drafted (see `annual-progress-report-template.md`)
- [ ] 500 paying analyst milestone: actual vs target
- [ ] Predictive crisis index v0 internal prototype
- [ ] Planet Labs partnership negotiation initiated
- [ ] Full vision document updated for Year 2
- [ ] Investor narrative updated with Year 1 traction data

## Milestone Alert Triggers

If any of the following are missed by >50% at the quarter-end review, a formal replanning session is required:

- MAU target missed by >50%
- Enterprise pilots: 0 signed by end of Q3
- AI Copilot activation rate: <20%
- Press citations: 0 by end of Q2
