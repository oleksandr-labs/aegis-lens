# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

> Use for commercial discussions where **both** parties exchange confidential
> information. This is the default NDA for partnerships, integrations, and
> evaluation deals.

---

**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of
`[EFFECTIVE_DATE]` (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

- `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]`, a `[AEGIS_ENTITY_TYPE]` with its principal place of
  business at `[AEGIS_ADDRESS]` ("**Aegis Lens**"); and
- `[COUNTERPARTY_NAME]`, a `[COUNTERPARTY_TYPE]` with its principal place of
  business at `[COUNTERPARTY_ADDRESS]` ("**Counterparty**").

Each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore `[PURPOSE — e.g., a potential commercial
relationship]` (the "**Purpose**") and in connection with the Purpose may
disclose to each other certain confidential and proprietary information.

### 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by one
Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other (the "**Receiving Party**"),
whether oral, written, electronic, or visual, that is designated as confidential
or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature
and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation, business
plans, financial data, customer and source lists, methodologies, software,
data-collection and verification techniques, and the existence and terms of this
Agreement.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does **not** include information that the Receiving
Party can demonstrate: (a) was lawfully in its possession without obligation of
confidentiality before disclosure; (b) is or becomes publicly available through
no breach by the Receiving Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party
without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of or
reference to the Confidential Information.

### 4. Obligations

The Receiving Party shall: (a) use the Confidential Information solely for the
Purpose; (b) protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses for its
own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care; and
(c) limit access to those of its employees, advisors, and contractors who have a
need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no
less protective than those herein. The Receiving Party is responsible for any
breach by such persons.

### 5. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is legally compelled to disclose Confidential
Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing
Party prompt notice and reasonable cooperation to seek protective treatment, and
shall disclose only that portion legally required.

### 6. No License; No Warranty

No license or other right is granted under any intellectual property by this
Agreement. All Confidential Information is provided "**as is**" without warranty
of any kind.

### 7. Return or Destruction

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request or termination of this Agreement,
the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential
Information, except that it may retain copies required by law or routine backup,
which remain subject to this Agreement.

### 8. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for `[TERM — default:
two (2) years]`. Confidentiality obligations survive for `[SURVIVAL — default:
three (3) years]` after disclosure, except that obligations with respect to
information constituting a trade secret continue for as long as it remains a
trade secret under applicable law.

### 9. No Obligation; No Partnership

Nothing herein obligates either Party to proceed with any transaction or creates
any agency, partnership, or joint venture.

### 10. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of `[GOVERNING_LAW — default: the State
of Delaware]`, without regard to conflicts of law. The Parties submit to the
exclusive jurisdiction of `[VENUE — default: the state and federal courts
located in Delaware]`.

### 11. Equitable Relief

The Parties agree that monetary damages may be inadequate for a breach and that
the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief without the
requirement of posting a bond.

### 12. Miscellaneous

This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject matter, supersedes prior
discussions, and may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties. If any
provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. This Agreement may be
executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
