# One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement (Inbound)

> Use when Aegis Lens **receives** confidential information and discloses little
> or none of its own — vendor demos under their confidentiality request,
> handling of candidate or source-provided material, evaluating a dataset.
> Aegis Lens is the **Receiving Party**.

---

**ONE-WAY NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of
`[EFFECTIVE_DATE]` by and between `[DISCLOSING_PARTY_NAME]` (the "**Disclosing
Party**") and `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` (the "**Receiving Party**" or "**Aegis
Lens**").

### 1. Purpose

The Disclosing Party wishes to disclose certain confidential information to the
Receiving Party in connection with `[PURPOSE]` (the "**Purpose**").

### 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by the
Disclosing Party that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would
understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances.

### 3. Exclusions

The standard exclusions apply: information (a) already lawfully known to the
Receiving Party without restriction; (b) public through no fault of the
Receiving Party; (c) received from a third party without restriction; or
(d) independently developed without reference to the Confidential Information.

### 4. Obligations

The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose,
protect it with reasonable care, and restrict access to personnel and advisors
with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

### 5. Source-Protection Carve-Out (Aegis-specific)

Where the Confidential Information identifies or could identify a human source,
witness, or vulnerable individual, the Receiving Party will apply its
source-protection and PII-redaction standards. **Nothing in this Agreement
requires the Receiving Party to suppress or decline to report information that
is independently obtained from public sources, nor to compromise editorial
independence.** Confidentiality applies to the Disclosing Party's non-public
*materials*, not to underlying facts lawfully obtainable elsewhere.

### 6. Compelled Disclosure

Same as the mutual NDA: prompt notice where legally permitted, minimum
necessary disclosure, cooperation to seek protective treatment.

### 7. No License; No Warranty; Return/Destruction; Term

No IP license is granted; information is "as is." On request or termination the
Receiving Party returns or destroys the Confidential Information (subject to
legal-hold and backup retention). Term: `[TERM — default: two (2) years]`;
survival `[default: three (3) years]`; trade secrets for as long as they remain
secret.

### 8. Governing Law; Equitable Relief; Miscellaneous

Governed by `[GOVERNING_LAW]`; exclusive venue `[VENUE]`. The Disclosing Party
may seek injunctive relief for breach. Entire agreement; amendments in writing;
counterparts and electronic signature permitted.

**Redline notes (internal).** Because we are the Receiving Party, scrutinize:
broad definitions of "Confidential Information" that sweep in residuals or

work-product* derived from public sources. Never sign a one-way NDA that fetters
