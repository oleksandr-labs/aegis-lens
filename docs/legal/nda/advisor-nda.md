# Advisor Confidentiality Agreement

> Use for advisors, fractional experts, and light-touch contractors. Pairs with
> the Advisor Agreement / consulting agreement (which covers equity, scope, and
> compensation). This document covers confidentiality and a narrow work-product
> assignment only.

---

**ADVISOR CONFIDENTIALITY AND WORK-PRODUCT AGREEMENT**

Between `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` (the "**Company**") and `[ADVISOR_NAME]` (the
"**Advisor**"), effective `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`.

### 1. Confidential Information

In the course of advising the Company, the Advisor will receive Confidential
Information (as defined in the Company's standard NDA). The Advisor shall hold it
in confidence, use it solely to provide advisory services, and not disclose it
during or after the engagement.

### 2. Work-Product Assignment (narrow)

Any deliverable the Advisor creates **specifically for the Company** under the
engagement (e.g., a written analysis, a model, a methodology document) is
assigned to the Company on creation. The Advisor retains ownership of
pre-existing materials, general knowledge, skills, and methods, and may grant the
Company a non-exclusive license to any such background materials embedded in a
deliverable.

### 3. Conflicts and Independence

The Advisor will disclose material conflicts of interest. The Advisor may advise
other companies, provided they do not use the Company's Confidential Information
for any third party. Advising a direct competitor on a competing product
requires the Company's prior written consent.

### 4. No Employment

The Advisor is an independent contractor, not an employee, and this Agreement
creates no partnership or agency.

### 5. Term

`[TERM — default: the duration of the engagement plus two (2) years for
confidentiality]`. Trade-secret obligations continue for as long as the
information remains a trade secret.

### 6. Governing Law; Miscellaneous

Governed by `[GOVERNING_LAW]`; venue `[VENUE]`. Equitable relief available for
breach. Entire agreement; written amendments; counterparts and electronic
signature permitted.

**Redline notes (internal).** Advisors often push back on the work-product
assignment (§2) — keep it narrow (deliverables made *specifically for us*), and
never claim the advisor's background IP. The conflicts clause (§3) is the one
that matters for advisors who sit on multiple boards; be explicit about which
competitors are out of bounds.
