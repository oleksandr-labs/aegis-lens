# Employee Confidentiality & IP Assignment Agreement

> Use for all new employees. Bundles confidentiality with present-assignment of
> work-product intellectual property. Execute **before** the start date, as part
> of the offer-acceptance package.

> **Required:** retain the DTSA immunity notice in §6 verbatim. Omitting it
> forfeits the company's right to exemplary damages and attorneys' fees in U.S.
> trade-secret actions against the employee (18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)).

---

**EMPLOYEE CONFIDENTIALITY AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT**

Between `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` (the "**Company**") and `[EMPLOYEE_NAME]` (the
"**Employee**"), effective `[START_DATE]`.

### 1. Confidential Information

The Employee will receive and develop Confidential Information (defined as in the
company's standard NDA, including source-protection data, verification
methodology, customer and source lists, and code). The Employee shall hold it in
strict confidence, use it only for the Company's benefit, and not disclose it
during or after employment, except as required to perform the role.

### 2. Present Assignment of Inventions

The Employee **hereby assigns** to the Company all right, title, and interest in
all inventions, works of authorship, designs, data, and other work product
conceived or reduced to practice, alone or with others, in the course of
employment or using Company resources or Confidential Information
("**Inventions**"). This is a present assignment effective on creation. The
Employee will execute documents reasonably needed to perfect and record such
rights and appoints the Company as attorney-in-fact for that limited purpose if
the Employee is unavailable.

### 3. Prior Inventions

Inventions made before employment and listed in **Schedule A** are excluded. If
no Schedule A is attached, the Employee represents there are no such prior
inventions.

### 4. Statutory Carve-Out

This assignment does **not** apply to any invention that qualifies for
protection under applicable state law (e.g., Cal. Lab. Code § 2870 and
equivalents): an invention developed entirely on the Employee's own time without
Company equipment, supplies, or Confidential Information, and that does not
relate to the Company's business or result from work performed for the Company.

### 5. Return of Materials; Moral Rights

On termination the Employee returns all Company property and Confidential
Information. To the extent permitted by law, the Employee waives moral rights in
the Inventions in favor of the Company.

### 6. DTSA Immunity Notice (do not remove)

> Under the U.S. Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b): An individual
> shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state
> trade-secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that (A) is made (i) in
> confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, directly or
> indirectly, or to an attorney; and (ii) solely for the purpose of reporting or
> investigating a suspected violation of law; or (B) is made in a complaint or
> other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if such filing is made
> under seal. An individual who files a lawsuit for retaliation by an employer
> for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to
> the individual's attorney and use it in the court proceeding, if the individual
> files any document containing the trade secret under seal and does not disclose
> the trade secret except pursuant to court order.

### 7. Whistleblower / Lawful Disclosure

Nothing in this Agreement limits the Employee's right to report possible
violations of law to a government agency, to cooperate with an investigation, or
to exercise rights protected by law. Such activity is not a breach.

### 8. Term; Governing Law

Confidentiality and assignment obligations survive termination of employment.
Governed by `[GOVERNING_LAW]`; venue `[VENUE]`. Severability and entire-agreement
provisions as in the standard NDA.
