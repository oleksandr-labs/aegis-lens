# Redline Playbook

> Internal. Tells the deal team what they may concede **without counsel** and
> what must escalate. The point of a modular, fair paper set is speed — most
> deals should close on standard terms. Track every concession in the deal
> record so we learn which clauses keep getting hit.

## Traffic-light guide

| Clause | 🟢 Concede freely | 🟡 Negotiate within limits | 🔴 Counsel required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Governing law / venue** | Customer's home US state; England & Wales; Germany | Other EU member states | Non-OECD jurisdictions; mandatory arbitration in unfamiliar fora |
| **Payment terms** | Net 30 → Net 45 | Net 60 (with finance OK) | Net 90+; milestone/usage-only billing |
| **Term / renewal** | Non-renewal notice 30 → 60 days | Opt-out of auto-renewal | Multi-year with mid-term price locks below floor |
| **Liability cap** | Cap = 12 months' fees (standard) | Cap up to 2× annual fees | Uncapped general liability; removing the consequential-damages waiver |
| **Indemnity (our IP indemnity)** | Add prompt-notice / control-of-defense detail | Add a super-cap (e.g., 2×) for IP indemnity | Broad indemnities beyond IP (e.g., general "any claim"); IP indemnity for Customer Data |
| **Warranty** | Reaffirm "performs per Documentation" | Add a short cure-or-refund SLA hook | Fitness/accuracy guarantees on intelligence outputs (**never** — outputs are assessments) |
| **DPA** | Use our DPA + SCCs as-is | Customer's DPA if substantively equivalent | Customer DPA with audit-on-demand, unlimited liability, or data-localization we can't meet |
| **Security** | Share SOC2/ISO summary | Complete a security questionnaire | Bespoke pen-test rights, on-site audits, custom controls |
| **Publicity** | Logo opt-out | Mutual press-release approval | — |
| **Source/editorial independence** | — | — | **Any clause fettering editorial independence or requiring suppression of public-source findings → hard stop, counsel + leadership** |
| **Acceptable use** | Clarify permitted internal uses | — | Removing the unlawful-targeting prohibition (**never**) |

## Hard "never" list

- Uncapped liability for ordinary breach.
- Guarantees of factual accuracy of intelligence outputs.
- Removing the unlawful-targeting / human-rights guardrails in the AUP.
- Any term that compromises source protection or editorial independence.
- Accepting customer paper wholesale without counsel.

## Process

1. Map the customer's redlines to this table.
2. 🟢 → accept and note. 🟡 → accept within the stated limit, log it. 🔴 → route
   to counsel with the specific clause and the customer's rationale.
3. Record final positions in the deal record so repeated 🔴 items can be
   pre-negotiated into v-next of the template.
