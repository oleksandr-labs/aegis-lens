**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is between
`[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` ("**Aegis Lens**") and `[CUSTOMER_NAME]` ("**Customer**"),
effective on the date of the first Order Form (the "**Effective Date**").

### 1. Definitions

"**Services**" means the Aegis Lens platform and any services described in an
Order Form. "**Order Form**" means an ordering document referencing this
Agreement. "**Customer Data**" means data Customer submits to the Services.
"**Documentation**" means the published product docs. "**Confidential
Information**" has the meaning in §9.

### 2. Services and Access

Subject to this Agreement and the applicable Order Form, Aegis Lens grants
Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Services
during the Subscription Term for its internal business purposes. Aegis Lens may
update the Services provided it does not materially reduce core functionality
during a paid term.

### 3. Customer Responsibilities; Acceptable Use

Customer is responsible for its users' compliance, for the accuracy of inputs,
and for maintaining credentials. Customer will not: (a) resell the Services
except under a signed reseller agreement; (b) reverse engineer except as law
permits; (c) use the Services to violate law or third-party rights; or
(d) use intelligence outputs to facilitate unlawful targeting of individuals.
The **Acceptable Use Policy** referenced in the Order Form is incorporated.

### 4. Fees and Payment

Customer pays the fees in each Order Form. Unless stated otherwise, fees are
invoiced annually in advance, due **net thirty (30) days**, non-cancelable and
non-refundable except as expressly provided. Late amounts accrue interest at the
lower of 1.5%/month or the legal maximum. Fees exclude taxes; Customer is
responsible for taxes other than Aegis Lens's income taxes.

### 5. Term and Termination

This Agreement runs until all Order Forms expire or terminate. Each
Subscription Term renews per the Order Form unless either party gives notice of
non-renewal at least `[NONRENEWAL_NOTICE — default: thirty (30)]` days before the
end of the then-current term. Either party may terminate for the other's
uncured material breach after `[CURE_PERIOD — default: thirty (30)]` days'
written notice, or immediately for insolvency. On termination Customer's access
ceases and the data-return/deletion terms of the DPA apply.

### 6. Intellectual Property

Aegis Lens owns the Services, Documentation, and all related IP, including
aggregated and de-identified usage data used to improve the Services. Customer
owns Customer Data. Customer grants Aegis Lens a limited license to host and
process Customer Data to provide the Services. **Feedback** is licensed to Aegis
Lens on a perpetual, royalty-free basis.

### 7. Warranties

Each party warrants it has authority to enter this Agreement. Aegis Lens
warrants the Services will perform materially in accordance with the
Documentation; Customer's exclusive remedy for breach is repair or, failing
that, termination and a pro-rata refund of prepaid unused fees. **EXCEPT AS
EXPRESSLY STATED, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND AEGIS LENS DISCLAIMS ALL
IMPLIED WARRANTIES.** Intelligence outputs are analytical assessments, not
guarantees of fact; Customer is responsible for independent verification before
relying on them for consequential decisions.

### 8. Indemnification

**By Aegis Lens:** Aegis Lens will defend Customer against third-party claims
that the Services infringe IP rights, and pay resulting damages finally awarded
or settled, subject to prompt notice and control of defense. Aegis Lens may
procure rights, modify, or refund. Excludes claims arising from Customer Data or
unauthorized use.

**By Customer:** Customer will defend Aegis Lens against third-party claims
arising from Customer Data or Customer's use in violation of §3.

### 9. Confidentiality

Each party protects the other's Confidential Information with reasonable care,
uses it only to perform under this Agreement, and discloses it only to those
with a need to know. Standard exclusions apply (public, independently developed,
lawfully received, previously known). Compelled disclosure permitted with notice.

### 10. Limitation of Liability

**EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS BELOW, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY IS CAPPED AT
THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE CLAIM, AND NEITHER
PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR LOST-PROFITS DAMAGES.** "Excluded
Claims" (uncapped or super-capped per the redline playbook): a party's
indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, Customer's payment
obligations, and a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 11. Data Protection and Security

Where Aegis Lens processes personal data on Customer's behalf, the **DPA
Addendum** applies. Aegis Lens maintains the security measures described in its
security documentation and the subprocessor list at
[`../subprocessor-list.md`](../subprocessor-list.md).

### 12. Publicity

Neither party uses the other's name or marks without prior written consent,
except Aegis Lens may list Customer's name and logo in a customer list unless
Customer opts out in the Order Form.

### 13. General

Governing law and venue per [`jurisdiction-variants.md`](jurisdiction-variants.md).
Neither party assigns this Agreement without consent, except in a merger or sale
of substantially all assets. Notices in writing. Force majeure excuses
non-performance (other than payment) for events beyond reasonable control. This
Agreement, its Order Forms, and addenda are the entire agreement and supersede
prior terms; **Customer purchase-order terms are rejected.** Order of precedence:
Order Form → addenda → this MSA.
