# Master Services Agreement (MSA) Bundle

Enterprise contracting package for Aegis Lens. The goal is to **close sales
without month-long redlines** by leading with a fair, modular paper set.

> **Disclaimer.** The base MSA must be counsel-drafted/reviewed before first use.
> Never paste a customer's paper without counsel — one bad indemnity or
> uncapped-liability clause can sink the company. The redline playbook tells the
> deal team what they may concede without escalation and what must go to counsel.

## How the pieces fit

```
Order Form  ──references──▶  Master Services Agreement (MSA)
                                   │
                                   ├── DPA Addendum        (when personal data is processed)
                                   ├── SLA Addendum         (per tier: Standard / Pro / Enterprise)
                                   └── BAA                  (only where US HIPAA applies — rare for us)
```

The **MSA** is signed once per customer and governs the relationship. Each
purchase is a short **Order Form** that references the MSA. Addenda attach as
needed.
