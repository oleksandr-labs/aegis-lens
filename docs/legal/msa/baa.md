# Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

> **Use is rare.** Only required where Aegis Lens processes U.S. **Protected
> Health Information (PHI)** on behalf of a HIPAA-covered entity or another
> business associate. This is uncommon for a conflict-intelligence platform — do
> **not** sign a BAA reflexively. If a deal genuinely involves PHI, escalate to
> counsel and confirm our processing is actually in scope before executing.

---

**BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT**

This BAA supplements the MSA between `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` ("**Business
Associate**") and `[CUSTOMER_NAME]` ("**Covered Entity**") and applies only to
Protected Health Information ("**PHI**") as defined under HIPAA (45 C.F.R. Parts
160 and 164).

### 1. Permitted Uses and Disclosures
Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the
Services, as required by law, or as permitted by this BAA, and not in a manner
that would violate HIPAA if done by Covered Entity.

### 2. Safeguards
Business Associate will implement administrative, physical, and technical
safeguards (including those of the HIPAA Security Rule for ePHI) that reasonably
protect PHI, consistent with the security measures in the DPA Annex II.

### 3. Subcontractors
Business Associate will ensure subcontractors that create, receive, maintain, or
transmit PHI agree to restrictions at least as protective as this BAA.

### 4. Breach Notification
Business Associate will report any use or disclosure not permitted by this BAA,
any Security Incident, and any Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay
and no later than the period required by law after discovery.

### 5. Individual Rights
Business Associate will make PHI available for access, amendment, and accounting
of disclosures as required by HIPAA, and make its practices available to HHS for
compliance review.

### 6. Return or Destruction
On termination, Business Associate will return or destroy PHI where feasible; if
infeasible, it will extend protections and limit further use to that which makes
return/destruction infeasible.

### 7. Term
Effective on execution and terminating when all PHI is returned, destroyed, or
protections extended.
