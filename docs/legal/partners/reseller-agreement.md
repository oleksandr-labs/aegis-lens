# Reseller Agreement

> For partners who **resell** Aegis Lens on our paper at a margin and route leads.
> The reseller buys at a discount and sells to end customers, who sign the Aegis
> Lens [MSA](../msa/README.md). The reseller may bill the customer.

---

**RESELLER AGREEMENT** between `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` ("Aegis Lens") and
`[PARTNER_NAME]` ("Reseller"), effective `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`.

### 1. Appointment

Aegis Lens appoints the Reseller as a **non-exclusive** reseller of the Services
in `[TERRITORY]` for `[SEGMENT]`. Non-exclusive both ways: Aegis Lens may sell
direct and through other partners.

### 2. Orders, pricing & margin

- The Reseller purchases subscriptions at **list less the margin** for its tier
  ([common-terms §1](common-terms.md#1-margin--revenue-share-schedule)) and resells
  to end customers.
- The Reseller sets the end price **but may not advertise or sell below the Aegis
  Lens published direct floor** (anti-channel-conflict). Breach forfeits the
  margin for the affected deal.
- Each end customer executes the Aegis Lens [MSA](../msa/README.md) (or an
  approved flow-down); the Reseller may not alter our terms.

### 3. Lead routing

Inbound leads in the Reseller's registered territory/segment are routed per the
deal-registration process; conflicts resolve to the first valid registration
([common-terms §1](common-terms.md#1-margin--revenue-share-schedule)).

### 4. Reseller obligations

- First-line sales and (if contracted) tier-1 support; escalate to Aegis Lens per
  the [SLA addendum](../msa/sla-addendum.md).
- Accurate representation of the product; no guarantees beyond our published
  warranties.
- Comply with the [Partner Code of Conduct](partner-code-of-conduct.md), incl.
  **export/sanctions screening and end-use restrictions** on every end customer.

### 5. Aegis Lens obligations

Provide the Services per the MSA/SLA, product/sales enablement, and back-line
support. Honor registered deals.

### 6. Billing & payment

The Reseller is responsible for billing its end customers and **pays Aegis Lens
regardless of whether the end customer pays the Reseller.** Reseller payment
terms: net `[30]` days. Tax per [common-terms §3](common-terms.md#3-per-region-tax-handling)
(note nexus implications of reselling in new regions — finance gate).

### 7. Term, termination & customer transition

Per [common-terms §2](common-terms.md#2-termination--transition). **On
termination, end customers transition to a direct relationship or an alternate
partner with no service gap** — the Reseller cooperates with handover.

### 8. Confidentiality & governing law

Mutual confidentiality; governing law per [MSA jurisdiction variants](../msa/jurisdiction-variants.md).
