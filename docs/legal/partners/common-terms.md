# Partner Program — Common Terms

> Shared terms referenced by all partner agreements: margin/revshare schedule,
> termination & transition, and per-region tax handling. Keeping these in one
> place avoids drift between agreement types.

## 1. Margin & revenue-share schedule

**Design principle:** reward partners who bring scale and own delivery, but never
let channel pricing undercut our direct sales motion. Margins tier by committed
annual partner-sourced revenue and by how much the partner does.

| Tier (annual partner-sourced ACV) | Referral revshare | Reseller margin | White-label discount off list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Registered (< `[$100k]`) | `[10%]` | `[15%]` | n/a |
| Silver (`[$100k–$500k]`) | `[12%]` | `[20%]` | `[25%]` |
| Gold (`[$500k–$2M]`) | `[15%]` | `[25%]` | `[30%]` |
| Platinum (> `[$2M]`) | negotiated | negotiated | negotiated |

Rules:
- **Referral** revshare is paid on net collected revenue for the **first
  `[12 months]`** of the referred customer (or per-deal as registered).
- **Reseller** margin applies to the partner's purchase price; the reseller sets
  the end price but **may not advertise below our published direct floor** (anti-
  channel-conflict). Violations forfeit the margin for the affected deal.
- **Deal registration** governs attribution: a partner registers a lead; if
  approved and the partner is materially involved, the deal is credited to them.
  Conflicts resolve in favor of the first valid registration.
- Margins/revshare are paid on **net** revenue (excluding taxes, refunds,
  credits, and chargebacks) within `[45 days]` of our collection.

## 2. Termination & transition

- **Term:** `[12 months]`, auto-renewing unless either party gives `[60 days]`
  notice.
- **Termination for convenience:** either party on `[60 days]` notice.
- **Termination for cause:** immediate on uncured material breach (`[30-day]`
  cure), insolvency, or a [code-of-conduct](partner-code-of-conduct.md) breach
  (some conduct breaches are non-curable — see that doc).
- **Tail / surviving commissions:** on termination without cause, referral
  revshare already earned continues to be paid for its remaining window;
  for-cause termination for partner misconduct may forfeit unpaid amounts.
- **Customer transition (critical):** on termination, **end customers are not
  stranded.** For reseller/white-label, customers transition to a direct Aegis
  Lens relationship (or an alternate partner) with continuity of service; the
  partner cooperates with data/account handover and stops representing itself as
  affiliated. Transition assistance period: `[90 days]`.

## 3. Per-region tax handling

- Each party is responsible for its **own income taxes**.
- **Indirect taxes (VAT/GST/sales tax):** invoices state amounts exclusive of
  indirect tax; the paying party adds applicable tax. For cross-border EU,
  reverse-charge applies where both parties are VAT-registered businesses (each
  supplies a valid VAT ID).
- **Withholding tax:** if a jurisdiction requires withholding on payments to the
  other party, the payer may withhold as legally required and provides
  certificates; parties cooperate to apply treaty reductions (W-8/W-9 or local
  equivalents on file before first payment).
- **Nexus / registration:** reseller and white-label motions can create tax nexus
  where the partner sells — **finance must confirm registration obligations
  before launching a partner in a new region.**
- Tax provisions are jurisdiction-sensitive; the per-region variant overrides
  this section where they conflict.

---

**Redline notes (internal).** Margin %s are starting points — Platinum is always
negotiated. The two non-negotiables: (1) the anti-undercut / direct-floor rule in
§1, and (2) the no-stranded-customer transition in §2. Tax nexus (§3) is a
finance gate, not a sales decision.
