# Referral Agreement

> For partners who **refer** leads. Aegis Lens owns the customer contract and
> billing; the partner earns revenue-share on closed referred deals. Lowest-touch
> partnership — no resale, no support obligation.

---

**REFERRAL AGREEMENT** between `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` ("Aegis Lens") and
`[PARTNER_NAME]` ("Referral Partner"), effective `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`.

### 1. Referrals & registration

The Referral Partner may submit leads via the deal-registration process. A
referral is **eligible** when: it's a new prospect not already in our pipeline,
the Partner registers it before we engage, and we approve the registration.
Eligibility and attribution follow [common-terms §1](common-terms.md#1-margin--revenue-share-schedule).

### 2. Roles

Aegis Lens controls the sales process, pricing, the customer contract
([MSA](../msa/README.md)), billing, and support. The Referral Partner makes
introductions and may assist but **does not** quote prices, sign on our behalf,
or make product commitments.

### 3. Revenue share

For each eligible referred customer that signs and pays, Aegis Lens pays the
Partner the **referral revshare** for that Partner's tier on net collected
revenue for the first `[12 months]`, per [common-terms §1](common-terms.md#1-margin--revenue-share-schedule),
paid within `[45 days]` of our collection.

### 4. Independent parties; no authority

The Partner is an independent contractor, not an agent, employee, or reseller.
No authority to bind Aegis Lens.

### 5. Compliance & conduct

The Partner complies with the [Partner Code of Conduct](partner-code-of-conduct.md),
including anti-bribery, accurate representations, and the export/sanctions and
end-use restrictions (critical for a conflict-intelligence product).

### 6. Confidentiality, term, tax

Mutual confidentiality ([NDA](../nda/mutual-nda.md) terms apply). Term,
termination, surviving commissions, and tax per [common-terms §2–3](common-terms.md).

### 7. Governing law

Per [MSA jurisdiction variants](../msa/jurisdiction-variants.md).
