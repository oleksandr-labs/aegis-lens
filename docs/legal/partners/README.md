# Partner & Reseller Agreements

> Standard channel-partnership paper so we don't draft bespoke contracts each
> time. **Margin schedules reward big partners without undercutting direct
> sales** — that tension is the design constraint for the whole set.

> **Disclaimer.** Counsel-reviewed starting templates, not legal advice.
> Channel deals interact with revenue recognition, tax nexus, and the end-customer
> [MSA](../msa/README.md) — loop in finance + counsel before signing a new
> partner type.

## Channel model at a glance

| Motion | Who owns the customer contract | Who bills the customer | Partner upside | Template |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Referral** | Aegis Lens (direct) | Aegis Lens | Revenue-share on referred deals | [`referral-agreement.md`](referral-agreement.md) |
| **Reseller** | Aegis Lens paper, resold | Reseller (buys at margin) | Resale margin + lead routing | [`reseller-agreement.md`](reseller-agreement.md) |
| **White-label / OEM** | Partner (embeds our platform) | Partner | Wholesale/usage pricing | [`white-label-oem.md`](white-label-oem.md) |
| **Embed attribution (newsrooms)** | N/A (attribution license) | N/A or low-fee | Free/low-cost embeds with credit | [`embed-attribution.md`](embed-attribution.md) |

Cross-cutting terms (margins, conduct, termination/transition, tax) live in
[`common-terms.md`](common-terms.md) and are referenced by each agreement so they
stay consistent.
