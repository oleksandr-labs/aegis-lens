# Embed Attribution Agreement (Newsrooms)

> A lightweight, free or low-cost license letting **newsrooms and researchers**
> embed Aegis Lens public outputs (maps, charts, verified-event widgets) in their
> reporting, **in exchange for attribution.** This is mission + reach + brand
> credibility, not a revenue motion — keep it frictionless.

---

**EMBED & ATTRIBUTION LICENSE** between `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` ("Aegis Lens") and
`[PUBLISHER_NAME]` ("Publisher"), effective `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`. May be accepted
via click-through for the standard (free) tier.

### 1. License

Aegis Lens grants the Publisher a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free (for the
free tier) license to embed and display **designated public outputs** via the
provided embed widgets/links, in the Publisher's editorial content.

### 2. Attribution (the consideration)

Each embed must display the required credit — **"Source: Aegis Lens"** with a
link back — kept visible and unaltered. Removing or obscuring attribution
terminates the license for that content.

### 3. Permitted use & integrity

- Editorial/journalistic and research use. No reselling the embeds as a data feed
  (that's a [reseller](reseller-agreement.md)/[OEM](white-label-oem.md) deal).
- **No alteration that misrepresents the data**, its confidence level, or its
  provenance. The Publisher may crop/style for layout but not change values,
  remove uncertainty indicators, or imply verification we didn't assert.
- Aegis Lens may update or correct embedded outputs (e.g., after a
  [retraction](../../security/data-incident-runbook.md)); embeds reflect the
  current corrected state.

### 3a. No endorsement

Attribution does not imply Aegis Lens endorses the Publisher's reporting or
conclusions, nor vice versa.

### 4. Rate & availability

Free tier: reasonable-use embed limits. High-volume or commercial syndication →
a paid tier or a separate agreement. Provided "as is" with no uptime guarantee on
the free tier (see [SLA](../msa/sla-addendum.md) for paid).

### 5. Term & termination

At-will; either party may stop. Aegis Lens may suspend embeds that breach §2–3.
On termination the Publisher removes active embeds within `[a reasonable period]`.

### 6. Misc

No personal data is exchanged for standard public-output embeds (so no DPA
needed). Governing law per [jurisdiction variants](../msa/jurisdiction-variants.md).
