# White-Label / OEM Agreement

> For partners who **embed or rebrand** the Aegis Lens platform (or components/
> APIs) inside their own product. Highest-trust, highest-scrutiny partnership —
> the partner owns the end-customer relationship and our brand is hidden, so
> end-use and quality controls matter most.

---

**WHITE-LABEL / OEM AGREEMENT** between `[AEGIS_ENTITY_NAME]` ("Aegis Lens") and
`[PARTNER_NAME]` ("OEM Partner"), effective `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`.

### 1. License grant

Aegis Lens grants the OEM Partner a non-exclusive, non-transferable, **revocable**
license to integrate and resell the `[Services / APIs / components]` embedded in
the Partner's `[PRODUCT]` under the Partner's brand, in `[TERRITORY]`, during the
term. No right to the underlying IP, models, or source.

### 2. Pricing

Wholesale/usage-based pricing at the white-label discount for the Partner's tier
([common-terms §1](common-terms.md#1-margin--revenue-share-schedule)), or a
committed-volume wholesale rate. Overage per the order schedule.

### 3. Branding & attribution

- Partner may present the embedded functionality under its own brand.
- **"Powered by Aegis Lens" / data-provenance attribution** is required where
  `[specified]` (and always where outputs are presented as verified intelligence
  — provenance integrity is non-negotiable; see §6).
- Partner may not imply Aegis Lens endorses the Partner's other products.

### 4. Quality, support & SLA

- Partner owns tier-1 support for its end customers; Aegis Lens provides back-line
  per the [SLA addendum](../msa/sla-addendum.md).
- Partner must keep the embedded version within `[N releases]` of current for
  security; critical security updates are **mandatory** and may be pushed.
- Partner must not degrade or misrepresent verification confidence or provenance
  in its UI.

### 5. End customers & flow-down

The Partner's end-customer terms must **flow down** protections at least as
protective as our [AUP](../msa/base-msa.md), the [DPA](../dpa.md) where personal
data is processed, and the **export/sanctions + unlawful-targeting prohibitions**
(§6). Partner remains liable for end-customer compliance.

### 6. End-use, export & ethics controls (critical)

Because our brand is invisible in this motion, the OEM Partner must:
- Screen end customers against **sanctions/denied-party lists**; no embargoed
  destinations or prohibited end users.
- Enforce the **unlawful-targeting / human-rights** prohibition on every end
  customer.
- Preserve source-protection and verification-integrity guarantees — no
  presenting unverified outputs as verified, no stripping provenance.
- Breach of §6 is a **non-curable** material breach (immediate termination) and
  is reported to Aegis Lens promptly.

### 7. IP & feedback

Aegis Lens retains all IP in the Services. Partner owns its own product shell.
Feedback licensed to Aegis Lens (perpetual, royalty-free).

### 8. Term, termination & transition

Per [common-terms §2](common-terms.md#2-termination--transition); plus license
**revocation** on §6 breach. On termination the Partner ceases embedding within
`[wind-down period]` and transitions end customers without stranding them.

### 9. Confidentiality, liability, governing law

Mutual confidentiality; liability cap per the [MSA](../msa/base-msa.md); governing
law per [jurisdiction variants](../msa/jurisdiction-variants.md).
