# Partner Code of Conduct

> Mandatory for **all** partners (referral, reseller, white-label/OEM,
> attribution). Incorporated by reference into every partner agreement. Some
> breaches are **non-curable** and trigger immediate termination — flagged below.

A conflict-intelligence platform reaches sensitive end uses through its channel.
The code exists to ensure partners uphold the same ethics, legal, and integrity
standards we hold directly.

## 1. Lawful & ethical use (non-curable if breached)

- Partners and their end customers must not use the Services to facilitate
  **unlawful targeting of individuals**, human-rights abuses, or any use that
  endangers civilians or protected persons.
- Partners must enforce the unlawful-targeting prohibition on every end customer
  they sign.

## 2. Export controls & sanctions (non-curable if breached)

- Comply with all applicable **export-control and sanctions** regimes (EU, UK,
  US OFAC, and others as applicable).
- Screen end customers against **denied-party/sanctions lists**; no sales to
  embargoed destinations or prohibited end users.
- Report any suspected violation to Aegis Lens promptly.

## 3. Anti-bribery & anti-corruption

- Comply with anti-bribery laws (e.g., FCPA, UK Bribery Act). No bribes,
  kickbacks, or improper payments to win or keep business.

## 4. Honest representation

- Accurately represent the product, its capabilities, pricing, and especially
  **verification confidence and data provenance** — never overstate certainty or
  imply verification we didn't assert.
- No guarantees beyond our published warranties.

## 5. Source protection & data integrity

- Uphold source-protection and PII-handling standards; never attempt to
  de-anonymize sources or strip provenance from outputs.
- Handle personal data only under the [DPA](../dpa.md) where applicable.

## 6. Brand & channel integrity

- Use Aegis Lens marks only as licensed; don't imply endorsement beyond the
  agreement.
- Respect the anti-undercut / direct-floor pricing rule
  ([common-terms §1](common-terms.md#1-margin--revenue-share-schedule)).

## 7. Security & confidentiality

- Protect any Aegis Lens confidential information and credentials; report
  security incidents involving the Services promptly per our
  [security incident process](../../security/security-incident-runbook.md).

## 8. Enforcement

- Curable breaches: `[30-day]` cure per the partner agreement.
- **Non-curable breaches (§1, §2, and §6-pricing/ethics as specified):**
  immediate suspension/termination, forfeiture of unpaid commissions for affected
  deals, and customer transition to protect end users.
- Aegis Lens may audit compliance on reasonable notice where an agreement permits.
